Author
Raj Patel



📄 Overview
The Order Report Dashboard provides an interactive visual summary of order data for the year 2018, offering detailed insights into orders, profits, revenue, and product categories across cities and states. Built for business decision-makers, this dashboard enables quick identification of top-performing products, regions, and time periods, helping optimize strategies for sales and profitability.



📊 Key Features

✅ Date Filter: Adjustable order date range to focus on specific periods.

✅ Category, City, State Filters: Drill down into specific product categories, cities, or states.

✅ KPI Cards:



Total Orders: 500

Total Profit: 653K

Total Revenue: 1M

✅ Profit by Year and Month: Line chart tracking monthly profit trends across 2018.

✅ Profit by City: Pie chart showing the distribution of profit across top cities (e.g., Kohima, Mumbai, Pune).

✅ Profit by Category: Bar and pie charts highlighting which product categories (Shoes, Phones, Clothes) contribute most to profit.

✅ Profit by State: Bar chart ranking states by profitability.

✅ Revenue, Product Cost, Profit Table: Summary table breaking down financials by product category.




🔍 Insights Delivered
Top Performing Category: Shoes lead with 203K profit, followed by Phones at 182K.

Top State: Maharashtra generates the highest profit (~0.2M).

Monthly Trends: Peaks seen in March and April; dips during mid-year, with recovery in Q4.

Regional Focus: Significant profit concentration in urban centers like Mumbai, Pune, and Kolkata.
